## Direction contract

THESIS: The page proves credibility the way a nameplate proves a machine's rating — precise stamped facts read at a glance, not narrative persuasion. Refuses the incumbent site's soft editorial-magazine treatment (italic serif, broadsheet hairlines).

OWN-WORLD: Condensed grotesk sans throughout; monospace reserved for data (role, platforms, dates, credentials). Graphite/steel/paper neutrals; one restrained amber spent only on the current/active state. Hairline rules with small tick marks. The nameplate is a typographic and spacing discipline, not a literal metal prop — no rivets, no embossed texture, no themed chrome.

STORY: A recruiter scans in seconds, reads rated facts (role, platforms, scope) instantly, then verifies through anonymized engagements, method, and credentials.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Thin nav rail top. Left: name and title in large condensed grotesk. Right: compact stamped-data block (role / platforms / location) in monospace, one amber tick marking the current role. Portrait integrated as real content, not filler. Hairline tick-marked rule beneath.
